01 · Examples

What can be automated, before and after.

If someone in your company spends hours a week copying data from one place to another, answering the same questions or filling in sheets by hand, there's a good chance that work can be done automatically.

Customer service

Repeat questions answered

Before

You answer the same questions about hours, prices and availability by hand.

After

Common questions get an instant answer. Only the rest reach you.

Enquiries

Enquiries that don't get lost

Before

Requests arrive by email, form and phone, and some get forgotten.

After

Every request goes into the CRM with source and date, and gets an automatic reply.

Documents

Invoices without copy-paste

Before

Each invoice's details are copied into Excel one by one.

After

Documents are read and the data goes into the sheet on its own.

Reports

The Monday report

Before

Every week someone pulls numbers from several sheets to build the report.

After

The report is generated and sent on its own, always at the same time.

Bookings

Fewer missed appointments

Before

Reminders to customers are sent by hand, when there's time.

After

Every customer gets an automatic confirmation and reminder.

Integrations

Tools that talk to each other

Before

The same information is typed into three different places.

After

You type it once and the other tools update themselves.

New clients

Automatic client onboarding

Before

Every new client means creating folders, records and tasks by hand.

After

Everything is created the moment the client signs up.

Portals

Portals with no integration

Before

Documents downloaded one by one from portals that connect to nothing.

After

A bot logs into the portal, downloads and organises the documents for you.

Every automation is designed around your process and the tools you already use.

04 · FAQ

About automations, in a few words.

Do I need a website to automate?+

No. Automations work with the tools you already use: email, spreadsheets, CRM, management software. The website is just one possible connection.

How much does an automation cost?+

It depends on the complexity and how many tools are involved. In the assessment I estimate the time it saves, so the decision is easy, and the price goes in the proposal.

Is my data safe?+

Automations use the company's own accounts and tools, with access only to what they need to work.

What if something fails?+

Automations are tested with real cases before they go live. How follow-up and adjustments work is set out in the proposal.

Where does AI come in?+

In tasks involving text: sorting emails, summarising documents, extracting data or drafting replies. Where it matters, a person always reviews before anything goes out.
