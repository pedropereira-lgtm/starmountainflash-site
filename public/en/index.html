I start by getting to know the business: what it sells, to whom, and where it is losing customers or hours. I review the current site, if there is one, the competition and the tools already in use. At the end you get a free visual proposal and, if you want to go ahead, a quote tailored to what you asked for.

08 · FAQ

Before you ask for a proposal.

How much does a website cost?+

It depends on the kind of site and what it needs to do. I start with a free visual proposal. If you like it and want to go ahead, I send a quote tailored to what you asked for.

How long does it take?+

The timeline is agreed before work starts. Landing pages are the quickest; online stores and automations take longer.

What does showing up on ChatGPT actually mean?+

When someone asks ChatGPT for a recommendation in your sector, the answer lists two or three names. The work is getting yours among them more often, and measuring it every month.

Do I need a website to automate?+

No. Automations connect to the tools the business already uses: email, Excel, invoicing software, CRM.

Do prices include VAT?+

There is no VAT to add: the business is exempt under article 53 of the Portuguese VAT code. The quoted price is the final price.

Do you only work with businesses in Covilhã?+

No. I'm based in Covilhã, but I work with clients all over Portugal. Meetings happen online.
