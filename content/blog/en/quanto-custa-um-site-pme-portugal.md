A professional website for a small or medium-sized business usually costs between €500 and €5,000. An online shop can reach €15,000. The difference isn't arbitrary: it depends on the type of site, what it has to do and who builds it.

This guide explains the reference prices, what makes the price go up or down, and what you should ask before accepting a quote.

## Reference prices in 2026

- **Landing page** (a single page with a single goal): €500 to €2,000
- **[Company website](/en/websites)** (several pages, to present the business): €1,000 to €5,000
- **[Online shop](/en/lojas-online):** €1,500 to €15,000
- **Custom platforms** (such as [StudyOS](/en/trabalhos/studyos): members' areas, subscriptions, internal systems): usually over €10,000

These ranges are wide on purpose. Two websites with "five pages" can cost very different amounts, depending on what sits behind them.

## What makes the price vary

1. **Number of pages and features.** A contact form is not the same as a booking system or a client area.
2. **Custom design or a ready-made theme.** A bought theme is cheaper, but your site ends up looking like thousands of others.
3. **Content.** If the copy and photos are ready, the project moves faster. If they have to be created, the cost goes up.
4. **Integrations.** [Connecting the site to a CRM](/en/blog/ligar-formulario-site-crm-email), an invoicing program or a payment system adds work and value.
5. **SEO and AI readiness.** A site designed to [show up on Google](/en/blog/como-aparecer-google-seo-pme-portugal) and in ChatGPT's answers needs structure, technical data and well-organised content.
6. **Who builds it.** Agencies, freelancers and drag-and-drop platforms differ widely in cost, timeframe and level of support.

## The costs that don't always appear in the quote

- **Domain:** a `.pt` costs between €5 and €15 a year. It should always be registered in your company's name.
- **Hosting:** anywhere from a few euros to a few tens of euros a month, depending on the type of site.
- **Maintenance:** updates, backups and small changes. This usually comes to around €20 to €100 a month.
- **Shop platform:** shops on Shopify or similar solutions have a monthly subscription and payment fees.
- **VAT:** check whether the price quoted includes VAT. The difference can be 23%.

## Warning signs in a quote

- It doesn't say what is included, only the final price.
- It has no delivery date.
- The domain is registered in the name of whoever builds the site, not your company.
- It says nothing about mobile, speed or SEO.
- It doesn't say what happens after launch.

## What to ask before accepting

1. What exactly is included, page by page?
2. What is the timeframe, and what happens if it isn't met?
3. How many rounds of changes are included?
4. Will the domain, hosting and logins be in my name?
5. How much does it cost to maintain the site after launch?
6. Can I update the content myself?

## How much you should invest in your case

- **Just starting out or want to test a service?** A well-made landing page is enough. It is quick, affordable and focused on generating enquiries.
- **Offer several services and have clients looking for you on Google?** You need a company website with one page per service and content that answers your clients' questions.
- **Sell products?** An online shop, even if it starts small.

A cheap website that brings in no enquiries always ends up costing more than one designed to sell.
