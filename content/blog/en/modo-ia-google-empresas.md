In October 2025 Google launched AI Mode in Portugal: a tab on the results page where you type a long question and get a written answer, with links to a few sites. On Google Trends, "modo ai" and "modo ia" are among the AI-related searches that grew the most in Portugal over the past year. People are finding out they can search this way, and that changes how a company gets found.

**Short answer:** AI Mode relies on the same Google search systems to choose the pages it cites. A company shows up there when its site is indexed, answers customers' questions clearly and is confirmed by other sources. Since June 2026, Search Console has a report showing how many times your site appeared in Google's AI answers.

## How searching changes

In a regular search, someone types "aluminium window frames Paredes". In AI Mode they write something like "I need to replace the windows in a warehouse in Paredes, which frames handle the cold best and who does this locally". Google says questions in AI Mode are two to three times longer than traditional searches.

To answer, Google splits the question into several topics and runs many searches at once: frame types, insulation, local companies. Then it pulls everything together into one answer. So a page can be cited for answering one part of the question well, even if it would never rank first for a short search.

When Google isn't confident enough in the written answer, it shows regular results instead. Google itself admits AI Mode still gets things wrong.

## AI Mode, AI Overviews and ChatGPT

AI Overviews are the summary that appears at the top of regular results for some searches. AI Mode is a separate tab, where the person can keep the conversation going with follow-up questions. ChatGPT is a different tool, with different crawlers and rules, which I covered in [what ChatGPT needs before it recommends your company](https://starmountainflash.pt/en/blog/aparecer-no-chatgpt-empresa-portugal).

For Google, the rules are the same in both cases: no special files and no new markup. A page that can appear in regular search can be used in AI answers.

## What my own site shows

I looked at the generative AI report in Search Console for starmountainflash.pt. Between August and October 2026, the site appeared 41 times in Google's AI answers. The home page accounted for 35 of those and the StudyOS case study for 6. Blog posts got one or two each, because most of them are only a few weeks old.

These are small numbers from a site that is a few months old, but they say two things. The home page is used as a summary of who I am and what I do, so it has to say that right at the start, without vague wording. And the case study with the most technical detail was cited more often than more generic pages. AI prefers pages that explain how something was built.

Almost all of these appearances came from Portugal, and 37 of the 41 were on desktop, which points to searches made during working hours.

## How to check whether your company already appears

In Search Console, open **Performance** and look for **Generative AI**. The report shows how many times the site appeared in AI Overviews and AI Mode, by page, country, device and day. It doesn't show the questions people asked. If the report isn't there, the most likely reason is that the site doesn't have enough appearances yet.

Then test by hand. Open a private window, go to the AI Mode tab and ask the questions your customers would ask, in their words and with the full context: the area, the type of job, the deadline. Note who shows up and repeat a month later. Answers vary depending on who is searching and from where, so look at the trend rather than a single result.

## What helps a page get cited

The starting point is technical: if the site isn't in Google's index, it won't show up anywhere. If that's your case, start with [why your site doesn't show up on Google](https://starmountainflash.pt/en/blog/site-nao-aparece-no-google).

After that, content is what counts. One page per service, with the answer to the main question in the first paragraph. Concrete information AI can repeat: which areas you cover, how long things take, what the process looks like, what kind of clients you usually work with. Case studies with detail on what was done and why. And FAQs written in your customers' words.

Outside the site, consistency is what counts. Your Google Business Profile, your [reviews](https://starmountainflash.pt/en/blog/avaliacoes-google-pedir-e-responder) and the directories where the company appears have to say the same thing as the site. When information contradicts itself, AI merges the versions and gets the answer wrong.

## What you can't control

There is no way to guarantee your company appears in an answer. Google changes its systems without notice, and the same question can give different answers to different people.

It's also possible to appear and not get the click. If the written answer already settles the question, the person may stop there. That's why, beyond clicks, it's worth asking people who contact you how they found you.
